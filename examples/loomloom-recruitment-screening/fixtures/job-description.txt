Backend Engineer

Responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate production backend services and REST APIs.
- Improve service reliability, latency, and observability.
- Collaborate with product and infrastructure teams on delivery.
- Review code and contribute to engineering standards.

Must-have requirements
- At least three years of production software engineering experience.
- Strong Python experience.
- Experience designing REST APIs and working with relational databases.
- Evidence of owning production delivery or operations.

Preferred qualifications
- Docker or Kubernetes experience.
- Experience with a major cloud provider.
- Demonstrated performance or reliability improvements with measurable results.

Screening safeguards
- Evaluate only job-related evidence contained in the resume.
- Do not use protected characteristics or proxy attributes.
- Missing information must be treated as unknown rather than assumed negative.
